# Guardian-AI Regret Engine Policy

This demo knowledge base documents how Guardian-AI explains pricing regret decisions generated by the repository model. It is synthetic project knowledge for local development and testing.

## Regret Score

Guardian-AI estimates demand for the submitted price and for nearby counterfactual prices. Predicted revenue is calculated as price multiplied by predicted units sold. Regret is the positive gap between the best predicted counterfactual revenue and selected predicted revenue.

## Risk Bands

Decision quality is mapped to a dashboard risk level. GOOD maps to LOW risk when regret percentage is below 5 percent. QUESTIONABLE maps to MEDIUM risk when regret percentage is at least 5 percent and below 15 percent. HIGH_REGRET maps to HIGH risk when regret percentage is at least 15 percent.

## Required Explanation Fields

Every explanation should include the selected price, best counterfactual price, regret amount, regret percentage, risk level, key factors, assumptions, uncertainties, and cited retrieved evidence. Explanations should not claim evidence that was not retrieved.
